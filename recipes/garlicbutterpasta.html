Garlic Butter Pasta

Description

Here is a super simple and fast 10-minute Garlic Butter Pasta recipe that uses basic pantry ingredients.

Ingredients:

  • 200g spaghetti or any pasta you like
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley (chopped, for garnish)

Steps:

  1. Boil the pasta: Cook your pasta in a large pot of salted boiling water according to the package instructions. Reserve 2 tablespoons of pasta water before draining.
  2. Make the garlic butter: In a separate large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant (do not let it burn).
  3. Combine: Toss the drained pasta into the skillet with the garlic butter. Add the reserved pasta water and toss well to coat.
  4. Season and serve: Sprinkle with salt, black pepper, and parmesan cheese. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve hot.
